## Choosing and reusing evidence
The course core sequence is Labs 1–7; optional Labs 8–15 retain their status. A book example does not silently assign a later course lab. For source inspection or a decision exercise, use the specified reading subset. Reuse earlier results when revision, dataset, configuration and claim remain applicable; rerun when they change.

The [reference appendices](reference/README.md) provide compact essentials and blank records. The [scope guide](SCOPE_AND_TERMINOLOGY.md) explains evidence categories and important terminology.
