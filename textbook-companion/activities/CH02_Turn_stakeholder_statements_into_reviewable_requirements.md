## Analyze

1. How does your interpretation differ from the source?
2. Who may agree the requirement?
3. What observation would satisfy it?
4. Which assumption could reverse the proposed choice?

## Evidence limitation

Fictional stakeholder statements are not executed-system evidence or proof of legal obligations. State the most useful additional evidence.
