## Why you are doing this

Recommend stages, acceptance gates, handover responsibilities, and review triggers after change or incident. The activity connects an inspectable result to a bounded architectural claim. Preserve the distinction between what is observed, inferred, proposed, and still unknown.

## Predict before execution or inspection

1. Which claim most exceeds available evidence?
2. Which changed requirement would invalidate a prior passing test?

Write a reason for each prediction. If you have already seen the result, say so rather than reconstructing a prediction afterward.

## Interpret

1. Can the reviewer trace both forward and backward?
2. Which items are demonstrated, simulated, proposed, or untested?
3. Are dependencies separate from copies?
4. What may proceed, what is deferred, and who decides?

## Evidence critique

State what your evidence supports, what it does not establish, and which additional observation would most strengthen the conclusion. Lab evidence does not establish the entire proposed retail architecture; simulated lending observations are not retail execution.
