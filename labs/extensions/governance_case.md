# Optional Lab 15: Governance applicability and stewardship case

**Connection:** SLOs 1, 2, 5 and 6; Modules 1, 6–7. **Time:** 60–90 minutes.
This reading and reasoning activity needs no installation or external account.
It extends the required short discussion in the module map; it does not replace it.

## Fictional scenario and supplied facts

RetailBridge is a fictional US retailer proposing support-priority predictions.
It stores order identifiers, contact details and free-text support messages for
customers in several countries. A proposed EU-facing shop would deliberately market
to customers in the EU. The launch has not occurred. Some messages voluntarily
mention medical conditions. The retailer does not operate a health plan or clinic;
no relationship with a covered healthcare organization has been established.

A vendor proposes receiving full support messages for model improvement. The purpose,
retention period, location of processing, subcontractors and deletion behavior have
not been agreed. The operations manager wants to retain records indefinitely in case
they become useful. The team has aggregate accuracy results but no subgroup evaluation.
These are scenario facts and gaps, not a real organization's compliance assessment.

## Read primary sources

- [European Commission: GDPR application](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/application-gdpr_en).
- [GDPR official text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj): read Articles 3
  (scope), 5 (principles), 6 (lawfulness), and 28 (processors); identify other relevant
  provisions only when justified by your scenario analysis.
- [HHS: covered entities and business associates](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/covered-entities/index.html).
- [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) for voluntary
  governance guidance; distinguish it from legislation.

Record access dates and exact sections. Sources were selected on 2026-09-18;
recheck current applicability when adapting the exercise. Do not equate health-related
text with automatic HIPAA coverage, or assume a US headquarters excludes GDPR.
The sources establish scope tests, not the final answer for every hypothetical fact.

## Guided example

Claim: "Indefinite retention is justified because the data may be useful."
Question to investigate: which specified purpose requires which records, for how long?
Possible proposed control: a purpose-specific retention schedule with owner approval.
Missing evidence: approved periods, deletion tests, holds and downstream-copy handling.
This example illustrates a reasoning pattern; it is not a legal retention period.

## Independent transfer and stewardship decision

The vendor now requests permission to use the same messages to screen refund requests.
Write a one-page approve/conditionally approve/defer/reject memo addressing purpose,
minimum necessary fields, vendor responsibilities, affected people, retention,
transparency, human review and appeal. Include an exception register entry with
request, rationale, approver role, expiry/review trigger and unresolved risk.
Do not invent facts to justify approval; requesting more information is valid.

## Assessment and submission

Submit the matrix, memo and source list privately. No completion screenshot is needed.
Rubric: scope and source reasoning 30%; control/evidence mapping 30%; accountable
lifecycle decision 25%; clarity and uncertainty 15%. Strong work separates established
facts from assumptions and identifies how to validate a proposed control.
This educational case does not certify regulatory compliance or substitute for legal review.
