# Instructor start here

1. **Select a baseline.** Follow [release management](docs/release_management.md)
   to select an exact revision with a passing workflow. Record that revision in
   your LMS; do not treat changing `main` as a semester release.
2. **Rehearse as a learner.** Follow [Student start](STUDENT_START_HERE.md) on
   disposable Ubuntu, then the [validation commands](docs/validation.md) for the
   activities you will teach. Practice one recovery case from the lab guides.
3. **Map the offering.** Use the [required course path](docs/course_checklist.md)
   with your authorized syllabus. The core lab sequence is Labs 1–7; companions
   and capstone complete the required course path. Labs 8–15 remain optional.
   Consult [authorship](AUTHORS.md), [alignment](docs/syllabus_alignment.md),
   [module map](docs/module_learning_map.md) and [session notes](docs/teaching_sessions.md)
   as references, not parallel assignment checklists.
4. **Calibrate teaching and feedback.** Rehearse a session with the
   [delivery plan](docs/delivery_plan.md), then assess a
   [fictional example](docs/assessment_examples.md). Complete the novice and
   replacement-instructor [pilots](docs/adoption_checklist.md); record actual
   timing and difficulties in the [pilot form](docs/pilot_record.md).
5. **Configure and freeze the offering.** Publish dates, grade weights,
   accommodations, support and submission routes privately in the LMS.
   Keep instructor calibration submissions private. Record known limits and
   pilot status before publishing a versioned teaching release.

`course.py all` rehearses Labs 1–9, including optional activities. It does not mean
all nine are required or that all optional labs have run. The [lab index](labs/README.md)
lists each route. Automated checks cannot grade reasoning or certify a syllabus.
