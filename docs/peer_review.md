# Peer review and revision

Exchange work privately through the approved course system. Independent learners
may use this as a self-review. Allow 15 minutes for review and 10 for a response.
Never exchange configuration files or unredacted logs.

1. Identify one claim and its supporting artifact. Can you trace it to a command,
   model, decision or observation?
2. Classify the claim: demonstrated, simulated, proposed or untested. Is the author's
   label supported? Identify one overstatement or confirm a well-bounded claim.
3. Explain one missing reproduction detail without executing unfamiliar code.
4. Ask one question about an affected stakeholder or AI lifecycle decision.
5. Recommend one feasible improvement, with a reason and a check for completion.

Author response: record accepted/deferred feedback, reason, revision and remaining
uncertainty. Include the review and response in the private capstone evidence appendix.
Assess the quality of feedback and response, not agreement or personal preference.
